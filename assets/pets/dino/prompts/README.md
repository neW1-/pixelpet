# Dino LED Pet Prompts

Create a tiny friendly green dinosaur in 32x32 pixel art on a black background. Keep the silhouette stable across states and reserve the top-left corner for status bubbles when needed.

States:

- `idle`: sleeping Dino with small blue Z marks.
- `working`: Dino pacing left and right.
- `needs_input`: Dino waving with a blue exclamation bubble.
- `permission_required`: Dino with an orange question bubble.
- `done`: happy Dino with a green or white check mark.
- `failed_offline`: sad Dino with a red X or disconnected cue.

Export each state as a 32x32 animated GIF.
